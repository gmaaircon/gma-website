**Client:** Scottish NHS board<br>
**Sector:** Healthcare / education<br>
**Service:** Air conditioning installation and commissioning

## The brief

The library in a hospital education centre needed reliable cooling to keep the space comfortable for the staff and students who study and train there. The board asked GMA for a solution that would be neat, robust and easy to maintain over the long term.

## What we did

- Supplied and installed **two Daikin high-wall split air conditioning systems**
- Mounted the outdoor units at ground level on **rubber anti-vibration mounts** to keep noise and vibration down
- Ran all interconnecting pipework and cabling on **medium-duty cable tray, double-layered for mechanical protection** – important in a busy hospital environment
- Installed external condensate drain pipes with protective tray work
- **Pressure tested with oxygen-free nitrogen** and evacuated to below 2 Torr before charging, in line with good F-Gas practice
- Commissioned and fully tested both systems, recorded all commissioning data and completed a full client handover

## The result

The library now has dependable, efficient cooling. The work was completed over three site visits with no issues reported at handover, and the board has full commissioning records for its asset register and future maintenance.
