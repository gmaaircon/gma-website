Many building owners and facilities managers don't realise their air conditioning needs a statutory energy inspection. Under the Energy Performance of Buildings Directive, every air conditioning system with a combined effective rated output of **more than 12kW** must have a valid TM44 inspection report.

## Does the 12kW threshold apply per unit?

No – it is the **combined** cooling output of all the units in the building. An office with four 4kW split systems is over the threshold, even though no single unit is.

## How often is an inspection needed?

At intervals not exceeding **five years**. In Scotland the interval can be as short as **three years** where systems are assessed as poorly maintained or inefficient.

## Who is responsible?

The "responsible person" – usually the building owner, occupier or whoever controls the air conditioning system. Failure to produce a valid report on request can result in a fixed penalty of **£300 per offence**, which Trading Standards can repeat.

## What does the report include?

- An assessment of system efficiency and sizing against the cooling load
- The maintenance condition of the equipment
- Controls and system set-up
- Practical no-cost and low-cost recommendations to reduce energy use and running costs
